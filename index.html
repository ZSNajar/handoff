Handoff

One golden egg. Four jobs. One song.

Each job sings you a little rhythm. Tap the screen or press Space to sing it back. A pink ring shows when to tap whenever a rhythm is new. Your hit passes the egg along, and the song never stops. Tap anywhere to sing each rhythm back. A pink ring shows when.

Sound on, it's half the game.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger stage.
